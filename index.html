About the Residence

Designed for
Refined Living

The Evara is a singular residential address — one thoughtfully designed block of 40 premium 4 BHK homes across 10 floors, each offering 4,140 sq ft of beautifully planned living space.

Within the compound, a landscaped garden, a serene temple, and a peaceful gazebo create an atmosphere of calm — while 24/7 security and uninterrupted water supply ensure complete peace of mind, every day.
